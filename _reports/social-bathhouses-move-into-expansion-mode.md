The bathhouse category is moving beyond sauna and cold plunge access.




The stronger new operators are positioning bathhouses as urban third places: part recovery space, part social club, part hospitality venue and part emotional reset.




Bathhouse has raised $35M for U.S. expansion, with planned locations in Los Angeles, Chicago, Nashville, Philadelphia, Minneapolis, Stamford and additional New York-area markets. Othership has raised expansion capital for New York, with a model built around guided sauna, ice bath, breathwork and emotional wellness programming.




Lore Bathing Club has opened in NoHo with a membership-led contrast bathing model and hospitality/community operator DNA. Sauna House is building a more repeatable franchise-style contrast therapy model. Community Sauna Baths represents a different path, using community funding and local access rather than venture-backed expansion.




The category is splitting into several formats. Some operators are building large urban bathhouses. Others are developing guided group experiences. Some are standardizing contrast therapy into smaller repeatable units. Others are tying sauna culture to community ownership and neighborhood-level wellness.




The commercial question is whether recovery alone is enough.




Cold plunge, sauna and steam can be copied quickly. The more defensible models appear to be those with stronger programming, atmosphere, membership behavior, hospitality standards and social identity.




The next stage of the category will likely be defined by who can turn contrast therapy into repeat behavior, not who can install the best facilities.
