About

Field intelligence
at the intersection of
health, lifestyle, business,
and deeper vitality.

The New Health Clubs is researched and written by Jakob Rohrauer, an Austrian psychologist and Chinese medicine practitioner based in Asia.

His training spans a BSc in Psychology from the University of Vienna, a three-year diploma in Chinese Medicine and Acupuncture, and a three-year applied Gestalt therapy training — followed by five years of private clinical practice, including volunteer clinical work in Nepal. Alongside the clinical track: years of intensive Qigong and meditation training across Europe and Asia, and six years inside trading and investment communities — the world much of the premium wellness clientele comes from.

The editorial standard follows from that background: programs are evaluated on substance, not marketing.

New Health Access

The specialist sourcing and access desk built on this platform.

We place clients of travel designers, concierge firms, family offices, and private medical practices into vetted providers — destination longevity clinics, medical retreats, recovery programs, somatic and contemplative settings, and specialist practitioners.

The deliverable is selection: a client-specific brief, the reasoning behind it, and full coordination with the provider. Standard advisory commission structures.

The desk works with a limited number of partner firms.
