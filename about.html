About

The New Health Clubs is an independent intelligence platform tracking wellness-led physical spaces — longevity clinics, medical retreats, recovery clubs, social wellness clubs, destination sanctuaries, and the business models forming around them.

The index covers 116 operators across 97 cities. Signals track openings, expansions, investments, partnerships, and model shifts. Reports synthesize where the category is moving.

Tracking draws on operator announcements, trade press, public filings, and direct operator conversations. The editorial standard: programs are evaluated on substance, not marketing.

Who runs it

The platform is researched and written by Jakob Rohrauer, an Austrian psychologist and Chinese medicine practitioner based in Asia — BSc Psychology (University of Vienna), three-year diploma in Chinese Medicine & Acupuncture, applied Gestalt therapy training, five years of private clinical practice.
