About

Field intelligence
at the intersection of

I'm Jakob, originally from Austria.

My background combines psychology, holistic health, wellness, capital markets, and years of living across Asia and Europe.

I'm interested in the places where health, lifestyle, community, business models, and traditional vitality systems meet.

The New Health Club is my field map for understanding the operators, models, and relationships shaping this category. The public index maps the venues. Alongside it, through New Health Access, I maintain a private advisory layer of specialist practitioners and operator relationships — referenced here, never listed.
