A 50,000-square-foot Brooklyn spa and bathhouse combining Eastern European banyas, Finnish saunas, Turkish and Moroccan hammams, Japanese onsen-style pools, cold plunge, salt rooms, private treatment rooms, massage, body scrubs, lounge, and restaurant infrastructure. The venue brings global bathing traditions, spa treatments, recovery, food, and event-friendly hospitality into one large urban wellness destination.
