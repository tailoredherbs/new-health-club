New Health Access

The specialist sourcing and access desk built on The New Health Clubs.

Who it serves

Advisory firms, concierge desks, private medical practices, and family offices — and their clients. We act as the specialist back office for the wellness, longevity, recovery, and retreat category.

How it works
  1. You bring a client inquiry — a need, a constraint, a context.
  2. We return a client-specific selection brief: the right providers, the reasoning, and what to avoid for this client.
  3. We coordinate directly with the provider. Standard advisory commission structures; the client relationship stays yours.
What the vetting rests on

The desk is run by the platform's founder — a psychologist and Chinese medicine practitioner. Programs are evaluated clinically, on substance, not from brochures.

The desk works with a limited number of partner firms.
