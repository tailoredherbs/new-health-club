Next Health is scaling its health optimization and longevity clinic model through franchising. Business Wire reported that the company had passed 50 territories sold in 2024 and set a target of 150+ open locations by the close of 2027, while Athletech News later reported more than 70 units sold and development underway across 13 U.S. states.

The company’s model combines recovery technology, health optimization, aesthetics and advanced longevity services in an urban clinic format. Next Health’s franchise materials list support across real estate, construction and design, marketing, sales, recruiting, finance, medical operations, training and regulatory compliance, with franchise partner requirements including $600K minimum liquid capital and $1.5M net worth.
