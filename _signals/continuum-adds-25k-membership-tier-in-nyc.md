Continuum is adding an entry-level membership tier starting at $25,000 per year at its Greenwich Village flagship from January 2026. The club is located at 676 Greenwich St in New York and operates with limited, invitation-only membership.

The company’s model combines diagnostics, biological data, movement, recovery and expert-led programming. Continuum lists bloodwork, sleep data, VO2 Max, DEXA, movement analytics, wearable feedback loops and AI-supported planning as part of its precision wellness system, with services including private training, physical therapy, massage, red light therapy, float therapy, hyperbaric oxygen, cold plunge and sauna.
