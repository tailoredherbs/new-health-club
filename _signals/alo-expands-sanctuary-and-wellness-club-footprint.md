Alo opened its first Australian sanctuary at Chatswood Chase in Sydney, with the store officially launching on May 22, 2026. The 315 sq m location carries women’s and men’s apparel, footwear and accessories, and opened with programming including acro yoga, a live DJ, ear seeding, gifts with purchase, ALO Run Club and ALO Wellness Walks.

Chatswood Chase is the first of four Australian openings planned for 2026, with Bondi Junction confirmed as the next location. Separately, Alo Wellness Club has opened in London as the brand’s third invite-only wellness club, expanding beyond Los Angeles and New York with a format reported to include training studios, saunas, cold plunges, events and social spaces.
