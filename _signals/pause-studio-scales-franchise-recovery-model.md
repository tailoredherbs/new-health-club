Pause Studio is expanding its recovery studio model through franchising. Axios Charlotte reported that the company was opening a new location at the Arboretum in Charlotte on January 10, 2026, and said Pause had reached 17 locations across eight states.

The brand’s franchise model is built around a 2,600-3,000 sq ft recovery studio offering services including infrared sauna, cold plunge, contrast therapy, float therapy, cryotherapy, LED light therapy, IV drips, vitamin shots and compression therapy. IFPG’s 2026 franchise profile lists total investment at $880,600-$1,534,900, with a 7% royalty, 1% ad fee, $60,000 single-unit franchise fee and $500,000 liquidity requirement.
