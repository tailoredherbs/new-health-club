Bathhouse has raised $35M in a funding round led by Imaginary Ventures. The company currently operates large-format bathhouse locations in New York and is planning expansion into Los Angeles, Chicago, Nashville, Philadelphia, Minneapolis, Stamford and additional New York-area markets.  \[oai_citation:0‡The Fashion Law](https://www.thefashionlaw.com/bathhouse-raises-35m-as-social-sauna-sector-heats-up/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

The planned Los Angeles flagship is reported for the former Amoeba Music site on Sunset Boulevard, with an expected 85,000 sq ft footprint and a 2028 opening target. Bathhouse is targeting 10 U.S. locations by 2028.
