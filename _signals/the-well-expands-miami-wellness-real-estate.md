THE WELL is expanding its Miami wellness real estate platform with THE WELL Coconut Grove at 2855 Tigertail Avenue. The project follows THE WELL Bay Harbor Islands and is reported as a $650M development by Terra and THE WELL.

Public project materials describe an 8-story residential development with 194 residences, one- to four-bedroom layouts and an expected 2028 delivery window. The project includes branded wellness club amenities, retail and restaurant space, rooftop amenities and access for residents and THE WELL club members.
