Alo has opened an invite-only wellness club in London, expanding its physical wellness club format beyond Los Angeles and New York. Fitt Insider described the London site as the brand’s third invite-only wellness club.

The format combines fitness, recovery, hospitality and community programming. Reported amenities include training studios, saunas, cold plunges, curated events and creator-oriented social spaces, extending Alo’s apparel brand into an experiential club model.
