KX Chelsea, a premium health club in London, is investing £15 million in its most significant redevelopment since its launch in 2002. The project aims to create an integrated wellness model that combines training, recovery, and relaxation. This move is expected to enhance the club's appeal to premium clients seeking comprehensive wellness solutions.
