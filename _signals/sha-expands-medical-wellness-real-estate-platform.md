SHA Wellness is expanding its medical wellness platform through destination real estate and branded residences. The company operates in Spain and Mexico, with SHA Mexico opened in Costa Mujeres in early 2024 as its first outpost outside Spain, combining 100 rooms and suites with a residential component.

SHA Emirates is planned for AlJurf, on the coastline between Abu Dhabi and Dubai, in partnership with IMKAN. The project is positioned as a private healthy-living island with villas, apartments, penthouses, a health resort and SHA Wellness Clinic facilities, with public project materials listing a 2026 opening window.
