The Portal has successfully raised $5 million to establish a 15,000-square-foot club in Austin, Texas, set to open this fall. This development is part of a growing trend of members-only wellness concepts aiming to provide exclusive health and wellness experiences. The funding will support the creation of a space focused on 'human flourishing,' adding to the competitive landscape of premium wellness clubs.
