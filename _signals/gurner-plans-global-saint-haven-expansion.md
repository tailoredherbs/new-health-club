Gurner Group is planning to scale Saint Haven globally, with reports citing a target of 20-30 Saint social wellness membership clubs by 2030. The company is developing Sydney locations including Bondi Beach and North Sydney, with Dubai and U.S. expansion also planned.

Saint Haven combines private club, fitness, bathhouse, recovery and longevity programming in one membership model. The offer includes personal training, group fitness, executive health checks, IV infusions, hyperbaric oxygen, cryotherapy, red light therapy, steamrooms, cold plunge and magnesium pools, alongside a restaurant, cafe, elixir bar and business lounge.
