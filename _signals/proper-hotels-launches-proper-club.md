Proper Hotels has launched Proper Club as a members-only extension of its hotel brand. The company lists Santa Monica Proper and Austin Proper as current club locations, with Lake Tahoe Proper Club and Dallas Proper Club coming soon.

The membership combines fitness, wellness, cultural programming and hotel privileges. Santa Monica Proper Club includes access to fitness facilities, wellness classes, members-only spaces, cold plunges, dry sauna, recovery treatments, cultural events and selected hotel benefits including seasonal pool access, workspace access, member rates and complimentary Sunday night stays.
