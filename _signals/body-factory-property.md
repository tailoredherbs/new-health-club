Body Factory’s property arm says its first two lifestyle residence projects in Canggu and Uluwatu have been delivered, with the Uluwatu residence now fully sold out. The group is now presenting Body Factory Uluwatu as its third project.




The move shows how a well-known fitness and recovery brand can travel beyond the walls of the club. In Bali, where lifestyle, hospitality and property often blur together, Body Factory is using its name to give residential development a clearer wellness identity.
