Body Factory’s property arm says its first two lifestyle residence projects, Lifestyle Residence Canggu and Lifestyle Residence Uluwatu, have been delivered. The Uluwatu residence is listed as fully sold out.




The group is now presenting Body Factory Uluwatu as its third project. The project extends the Body Factory brand from fitness, recovery and café operations into branded property development.
