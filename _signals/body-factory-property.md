Body Factory's property arm says its first two lifestyle residence projects in Canggu and Uluwatu have been delivered, with the Uluwatu residence now fully sold out. The group is now presenting Body Factory Uluwatu as its third project.

This suggests a broader model shift: Body Factory is no longer only monetizing fitness, recovery, and lifestyle through memberships. It is turning brand trust and wellness demand into real estate value — a clear example of wellness-led spaces becoming anchors for property, hospitality, and mixed-use lifestyle development.
