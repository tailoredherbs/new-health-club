Nirvana Life is presenting The Sanctuary Hotel Suites as a part-ownership hospitality product connected to its Canggu wellness club. The offer includes operator-managed rentals, a shared rental pool and projected return language.




The investment product also includes lifetime access to Nirvana Life’s club facilities. The club access is part of the ownership package alongside the hotel-suite and rental-pool structure.
