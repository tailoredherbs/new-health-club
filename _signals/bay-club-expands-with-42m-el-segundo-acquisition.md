Bay Club has acquired a property in El Segundo for $42 million, marking the latest addition to its $1 billion real estate portfolio on the West Coast. This move is part of Bay Club's ownership-first strategy, which focuses on expanding its presence through strategic real estate investments. The acquisition highlights the growing trend of hospitality groups investing heavily in real estate to enhance their service offerings and market reach.
