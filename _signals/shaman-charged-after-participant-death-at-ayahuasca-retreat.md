A self-proclaimed shaman has been charged following the death of a participant who received both kambo (frog toxin) and ayahuasca at a wellness retreat in Australia. The incident highlights growing regulatory scrutiny around psychedelic and indigenous medicine practices in the retreat sector.

The case underscores liability risks for retreat operators offering plant medicine experiences, particularly when combining multiple substances or working with unlicensed practitioners. As psychedelic wellness grows mainstream, venues face increasing pressure to establish medical oversight and safety protocols.

This incident may accelerate regulatory frameworks around retreat medicine practices, potentially affecting how premium wellness destinations structure their consciousness and healing programs.
