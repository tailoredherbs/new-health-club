Lanserhof at The Arts Club is facing ownership and economic pressure after The Arts Club reported losses linked to the wellness clinic venture. City AM reported that the club recorded a £6.4M writedown on its stake in Lanserhof at The Arts Club, while The Times reported that The Arts Club swung to a £3.8M pre-tax loss.

City AM reported that The Arts Club reached a settlement with Lanserhof under which it became the sole shareholder of Artslans Urban Limited, the company behind the clinic venture. Lanserhof at The Arts Club remains positioned as a private wellness clinic in Mayfair, with medical services, diagnostics, 3T MRI, cardiology, fitness, restorative therapies and performance testing.
