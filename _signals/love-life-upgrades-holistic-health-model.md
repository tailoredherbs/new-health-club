Love.Life is upgrading its holistic health model after opening its flagship health and wellness club in El Segundo, California. The 45,000 sq ft location at 740 S Pacific Coast Highway in Plaza El Segundo combines medicine, nutrition, fitness, recovery and community under one roof.

The company was co-created by Whole Foods Market co-founder John Mackey and operates an integrated model built around functional medicine, diagnostics, health coaching, physical therapy, dietitians, fitness trainers and recovery services. Fitt Insider reported that Love.Life has added services, programs and partnerships while exploring expansion.
