Therabody has launched the CryoTherm Palm device, aimed at helping athletes manage heat during competition. This product has already seen use by England's national soccer team, highlighting its potential impact in sports technology. While primarily a consumer product, its adoption by professional teams may influence wellness venues focusing on athletic recovery and performance optimization.
