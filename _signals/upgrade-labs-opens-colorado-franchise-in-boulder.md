Upgrade Labs opened Colorado’s first franchise location on May 2, 2026, at 2100 28th St. in Boulder. The center is operated by franchise owners Michelle and George Amacker, who also operate four urgent care clinics in Boulder County.

The Boulder location uses body scans, movement assessments and other biometric inputs to create personalized treatment plans. Upgrade Labs describes its model as a Human Upgrade Center combining adaptive fitness, recovery and performance technologies for strength, recovery, energy, sleep and long-term vitality.
