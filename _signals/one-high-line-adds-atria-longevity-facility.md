One High Line in Manhattan has leased its five-story commercial space to Atria Health and Research Institute for a 52,000 sq ft longevity facility connecting the development’s two towers. The facility is expected to open in fall 2027.

Atria is a membership-based preventive medicine and longevity operator offering care across multiple specialties, with on-site testing and imaging. Reported services for the One High Line facility include whole-body MRI, genetic screening and advanced heart imaging, with memberships priced at roughly $20,000-$75,000 per year.
