REVO Studios is positioning itself in Barcelona as a biohacking studio for urban life. Its offer includes cryotherapy, hyperbaric oxygen therapy, red light therapy, PEMF, neurostimulation, massage and an app for booking, tracking progress and accessing community events.




The studio brings high-tech recovery out of the clinic or retreat setting and into a more regular consumer rhythm. It sits between boutique recovery and longevity care: more technical than a sauna studio, but still packaged as something people can book, repeat and fold into city life.
