REVO Studios is positioning itself in Barcelona as a biohacking studio for urban life. Its treatment menu includes cryotherapy, hyperbaric oxygen therapy, red light therapy, PEMF, neurostimulation and massage.




The studio also uses an app for booking sessions, tracking progress and accessing community features. The format combines recovery treatments, app-based scheduling and member engagement in a city-studio setting.
