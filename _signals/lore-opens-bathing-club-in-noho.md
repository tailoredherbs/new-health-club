Lore Bathing Club has opened at 676 Broadway in NoHo, New York. The 6,200 sq ft club spans two floors and includes a large dry sauna, infrared sauna, cold plunge and hammam-style heated benches.

The company was founded by James O’Reilly, associated with NeueHouse, and Adam Elzer, a hospitality and community concept operator. Lore operates a membership and session-based model, with current pricing listed from $25 off-peak single sessions, $55 peak single sessions, $149 three-session packs and monthly membership from $225.
