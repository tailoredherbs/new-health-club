Champneys has extended its long-term partnership with alternative investment manager Cheyne Capital through a new funding agreement. The renewed commitment includes a £32M capital expenditure facility for the company’s next phase of investment and expansion.

The funding is allocated for refurbishment and upgrades across Champneys’ portfolio of four spa resorts and two spa hotels, including bedrooms, public areas, spa and wellness facilities, food and beverage concepts and landscaping. Champneys also plans to use the facility to support domestic and international expansion through its management contract and franchising model.
