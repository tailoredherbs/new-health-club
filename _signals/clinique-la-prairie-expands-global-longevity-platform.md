Clinique La Prairie is expanding its longevity platform beyond its Swiss clinic into hubs, resorts and branded residences. The company lists recent Longevity Hub activity in Beijing and Dubai, alongside its AMAALA longevity health resort and branded residences in Saudi Arabia.

The AMAALA resort is planned as a 36,115 sqm health destination with 52 rooms and suites and 13 villas, built around medical care, nutrition, movement and wellbeing. Red Sea Global lists Clinique La Prairie Residences at AMAALA as 13 homes, while Clinique La Prairie’s Phuket page lists a Thailand health resort opening in November 2026 with 40 private pool villas and longevity programs.
