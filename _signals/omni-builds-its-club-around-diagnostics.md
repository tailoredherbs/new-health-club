Omni is framing its Bali club around a broad mix of diagnostics, performance testing and recovery. Its public materials mention blood biomarkers, body scanning, strength and power assessments, gut and blood testing, therapy, nutrition, gym facilities, café and co-working.

The offer gives members a more structured reason to return than access to equipment or amenities alone. A visit can begin with testing, continue through coaching and recovery, and later return to measurement. It is the wellness club as an ongoing health journey, not just a place to train, work or recover.
