Omni is framing its Bali club around diagnostics, performance testing and recovery. Its public materials mention 70+ blood biomarkers, blood pressure analysis, 3D body scanning, strength and power assessments, gut and blood testing, therapy, nutrition, gym facilities, café and co-working.

The club combines testing, training, recovery and workspace in one facility. The diagnostics layer creates a structured onboarding path for members and gives the operator a basis for follow-up sessions, retesting, coaching and nutrition services.
