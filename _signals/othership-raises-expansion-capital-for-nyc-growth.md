Othership has raised expansion capital for its New York expansion. Fitt Insider reported the round as an $8.5M SAFE note and said CEO Robbie Bent clarified that other reports citing $11.3M were inaccurate.

The company’s Williamsburg location is listed at 25 Kent Ave in Brooklyn and offers sauna, cold plunge and guided breathwork sessions. Othership is also planning a larger New York format, reported at roughly 13,000 sq ft, adding traditional bathhouse elements including steam rooms and warm pools alongside group classes, free flows and emotional wellness programming.
