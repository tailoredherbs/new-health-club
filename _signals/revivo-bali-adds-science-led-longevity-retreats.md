REVĪVŌ Wellness Resort Bali is adding science-led longevity programming in 2026. The resort lists a Holistic Longevity Retreat for September 21-27, 2026 and The Blue Zones® Retreat for October 10-16, 2026, combining longevity workshops, movement, nutrition, Balinese rituals and expert-led programming.

The retreats sit alongside REVĪVŌ’s Vitality Centre in Nusa Dua, which is positioned around longevity, biohacking and aesthetics. The centre lists precision diagnostics, biomarker testing, body composition analysis, VO₂ Max testing, continuous health monitoring, IV nutrient therapy, IHHT, PEMF, infrared sauna dome therapy, red light therapy and recovery packages.
