Therme Group is advancing Therme Korea-Incheon through an agreement with the Incheon Free Economic Zone Authority. The project is planned for the CS8 and CS9 sites at Songdo Golden Harbor, a commercial district in Songdo International Business District.

IFEZ reported that the project covers 99,041 sq m, with construction scheduled to begin in 2027 and opening targeted for 2031. Business Korea reported an estimated project cost of KRW 850B and a long-term land lease of up to 90 years, while Therme Group says the Incheon resort is expected to receive more than one million visitors per year.
