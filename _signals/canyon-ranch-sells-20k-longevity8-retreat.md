Canyon Ranch is selling LONGEVITY8, a four-day longevity retreat at its Tucson resort. The program is priced at $20,000 per person or $36,000 per couple and includes accommodations, meals and access to daily resort activities.

The program includes 18 one-on-one consultations, 15+ diagnostic tests and review of more than 200 biomarkers. Canyon Ranch materials list on-site physicians and licensed professionals, with testing and evaluations that include blood work, cancer screening, genetic testing, VO2 Max and DEXA Body Composition.
