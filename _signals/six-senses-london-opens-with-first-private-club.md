Six Senses London has opened at The Whiteley in Bayswater, marking the brand’s UK debut. The property includes 109 rooms and suites, 14 branded residences and Six Senses Place, the brand’s first private members’ club.

The wellness component includes a 2,300 sq m Six Senses Spa with a 20-metre indoor pool, magnesium pool, cryotherapy, flotation, recovery spaces, fitness facilities and a longevity clinic. Six Senses describes the magnesium pool as London’s first hotel magnesium pool.
