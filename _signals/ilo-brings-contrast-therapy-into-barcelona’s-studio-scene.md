ILO Studios has opened in Barcelona with sauna, ice baths, breathwork and recovery sessions. The studio is positioned around contrast therapy and guided recovery sessions.




Published pricing includes a €32 drop-in, €45 discovery pack, larger packs down to €19.80 per session and a €250 monthly unlimited membership. The offer gives Barcelona another dedicated recovery-studio format with clear session-based and membership pricing.
