ILO Studios has opened in Barcelona with a focused mix of sauna, ice baths, breathwork and recovery sessions. Its pricing is unusually clear for the category: €32 for a drop-in, €45 for a discovery pack, larger packs down to €19.80 per session and €250 per month for an unlimited membership.




The format feels closer to boutique fitness than to a destination spa or medicalised longevity clinic. It makes contrast therapy look like something that can fit into an ordinary urban week: booked in advance, repeated often and shared socially.
